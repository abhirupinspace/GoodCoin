- MC (k=10): mean 201 puppet-epochs and 442 fake deeds over 8 epochs to force 8/8 successes.
- Over 8 epochs (policy B, mean path): sock-puppets extract 7,187.5 GOOD; honest participants receive 7,187.5 GOOD they otherwise would not; burn avoided 1,993,014 GOOD; treasury unlocked 8,000 GOOD (puppets are also eligible voters for it).
- Max reward extraction per epoch with unlimited puppets, by ring size r (capacity = floor(50r/3) fake approvals, reward capped at 50 GOOD): r=3: 50 deeds → 2,500 GOOD, r=4: 66 deeds → 3,300 GOOD, r=5: 83 deeds → 4,150 GOOD, r=10: 166 deeds → 8,300 GOOD.
